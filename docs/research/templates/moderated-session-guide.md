## Moderator rules

- Explain that Grove is being tested; the participant is not.
- Ask for permission before recording.
- Do not collect identifying plant, home, location, or account data unless it
  is necessary and covered by consent.
- Do not teach the interface or defend the design.
- Use neutral follow-ups: "What are you thinking?", "What did you expect?",
  and "What would you do next?"
- Mark an intervention every time the moderator helps.
- Capture actions and words separately.

## Opening

"Thanks for helping us evaluate Grove, a plant-care product exploration. We
are testing the product, not you. Some parts may be incomplete or intentionally
wrong. Please work as you normally would and say what you are noticing. You
can stop at any time."

## Context interview

1. Tell me how you got started caring for plants.
2. What currently makes you consider yourself a new plant owner?
3. Tell me about the plants you currently care for.
4. Walk me through the last time you decided a plant needed attention.
5. What made you notice?
6. What did you check before acting?
7. Tell me about a recent time you were unsure what to do.
8. What did you do next, and what happened?
9. What tools, reminders, people, or routines are part of your current process?

Do not mention Grove features during this section.

## Closing

1. In your own words, what was Grove helping you do?
2. Which recommendation felt most trustworthy? What contributed to that?
3. Which recommendation would you verify elsewhere?
4. What, if anything, fit into a routine you already have?
5. What would make you stop using this?
