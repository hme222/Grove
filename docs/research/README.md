# Grove product research

This directory turns a general UX research guide into a small, decision-led
research system for Grove. The source is useful as a method primer; it is not
evidence that Grove solves a real user problem.

## Start here

1. Read [the Grove research operating system](grove-research-system.md).
2. Confirm the recruitment definition and decision owner in
   [the first study](studies/trusted-first-care-action.md).
3. Run sessions with the
   [moderated session guide](templates/moderated-session-guide.md).
4. Record observations in the [evidence log](templates/evidence-log.md).
5. Make a continue, revise, pause, or stop decision. Do not convert opinions
   into findings or findings into product claims without traceable evidence.

The source PDF is image-based. The local copy is preserved for visual
reference, while the Grove artifacts paraphrase the relevant ideas. Product
and pricing references in the guide are time-sensitive and should not be used
as current tool recommendations.

## What was integrated

The source guide contributes five useful rules:

1. Research is a repeated spiral, not a one-time gate.
2. Match the method to the question and product stage.
3. Separate attitudinal evidence (what people say) from behavioral evidence
   (what people do).
4. Use neutral, open-ended interview questions tied to the research goal.
5. Keep listening after release, but do not pretend prototype feedback is
   production usage.

Grove adds stricter product requirements: every study starts with a written
decision and threshold, AI behavior is tested in normal and failure states,
and every conclusion links back to raw observations.
