# FloraGrove lesson plan: 100 topics (outline for your review)

Written 2026-11-23. Status: **draft outline, no lesson content written.**

This is a list of topics only. It makes no care claims. Every lesson below is marked **source needed**: nothing is drafted until you approve the topic, and no lesson ships until each care or safety statement has a recorded source (project rule: do not claim advice is verified without one).

## How to read this

- **Audience:** new plant owners (see `docs/product/confirmed-context.md`). Titles are the questions a new owner asks, in plain words.
- **Format of one lesson:** about five cards, about three minutes, one idea, a "Why" line, and a "Sources for this lesson" section. Same player as today's six lessons.
- **Existing lessons (6):** Soil finger-test, Plant parent reset, Plant ER, Habit stacking, Your spot's light, Your window. They are counted in the 100.
- **Candidate sources (to confirm, not yet checked):** university extension guides, the Royal Horticultural Society, the ASPCA toxic and non-toxic plant lists and its poison-control line, and peer-reviewed horticulture papers where a claim needs one. The research step for each lesson will record the exact page.
- **Pet and kid safety lessons** get extra review: sourced to the ASPCA list and a veterinary or poison-control source, nothing from memory.

## Suggested order to write

1. **Start here** (10) first, so a new owner always has an obvious next lesson.
2. **Water** and **Light** next: the two biggest worries.
3. **Leaves and symptoms** after that; it feeds "Plant help".
4. **Pets and kids** with extra review, scheduled once a veterinary source is chosen.
5. The rest by what the survey and Study 001 show people ask for.
