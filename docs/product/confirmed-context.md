# Grove confirmed product context

Last confirmed: July 28, 2026

This file records founder-confirmed product context separately from research
findings and team assumptions.

## Audience sequence

1. **Primary MVP audience: new plant owners**
2. **First expansion audience: experienced plant collectors**
3. **Later professional and creator audiences:**
   - Florists
   - Plant influencers
   - Horticulturalists

These later professional and creator groups may have different jobs, business
models, permissions, and workflows. They must not be treated as one validated
segment or used to determine MVP success for new plant owners.

## Implications

- MVP discovery and usability studies recruit new plant owners.
- MVP decisions optimize for early care confidence, understandable next
  actions, learning, and safe recovery from mistakes.
- Experienced collectors should be studied later for advanced collection,
  knowledge, and workflow needs.
- Florists, influencers, and horticulturalists each require separate
  discovery before Grove designs professional or creator features.
- Evidence from one audience must not be generalized to another without a
  follow-up study.

## Still unknown

- The operational recruitment definition of "new plant owner."
- The primary job and problem severity for new plant owners.
- The business model, distribution strategy, and twelve-month objective.
- Shipped status, active usage, and validated outcomes.
- The product decision owner for the first scored study.

## Evidence status

The audience sequence is founder-confirmed product direction. It is not
evidence that these audiences experience a particular problem or will adopt
Grove.
