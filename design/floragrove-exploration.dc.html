Rebrand exploration · nothing in the app has changed yet

Grove becomes FloraGrove

Same system: Lime on Night and Root, Cormorant Garamond and Alegreya Sans, the hand-drawn line and the star. What changes is the name, and a new chance to tell its story: flora, the plants of a place, and grove, a small group of trees growing closely together.

Name check before you commit
  • FloraGrove is more distinctive than Grove, but it still contains "grove". It lowers the trademark risk rather than removing it. A trademark search by a lawyer in the app and software classes is the step that settles it.
  • "Flora" is crowded on the App Store: Flora, FlorA Plant Care, Flora Log, FloraAI, Flora AI, Flora Mind and FloraSnap are all plant apps. Expect people to mix you up in search. Leaning on Grove in the wordmark helps.
  • A quick web search found no existing FloraGrove brand. That isn't a clearance; check the domain, the App Store name and social handles before announcing.
