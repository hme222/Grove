Brand · added in v0.4

Wordmark
FloraGroe
FloraGroe
Light Alegreya Sans “Flora” (90% of the size) + bold Cormorant Garamond “Grove”. The two leaves are the “v”: outlined leaf for Flora, solid leaf for Grove. Leaves are Lime on dark, #3F5A1A on cream. Fixed artwork: design/brand/floragrove-wordmark-*.svg (outlined, no fonts needed). In the app: <FloraGroveWordmark />.
Mark and app icon
The mark is the v from the wordmark. App icon: Root #202D14 square, mark at 74%; Apple applies the rounded corners. Favicon and anything under 40px use a heavier outline (3.8 instead of 2.8) so the outlined leaf survives. Files: design/brand/icon-1024.png, favicon.svg.
Name
FloraGrove: one word, capital F and G, every time. Never “Flora Grove”, “Floragrove” or “FG” in copy. Possessive: FloraGrove’s.
grove / groves (lowercase) stay as the name of the community groups, and the community tab stays “Grove”.
Size and space
Minimum wordmark: 16px tall “Grove” on screen; below that, use the mark alone. Clear space around the wordmark: the height of the “G” on every side. Mark minimum: 16px with the heavier outline.
Don’t
Don’t recolour the leaves outside Lime / #3F5A1A, swap which leaf is outlined, set the wordmark in one weight, add a drop shadow, or put it on a photo without a Night or Root panel behind it. Don’t put the mark next to the wordmark: the wordmark already contains it.

Two primaries, two jobs

Quiet headline
Stop guessing when to water.
FloraGrove tells you what each plant needs today, from water and light to soil, and why.
Cormorant Garamond 500, 28–36px, max 24 characters per line, centred. The page's only headline. Name one concrete problem, then use the line under it to show the full scope. Avoid mood words (calm, quiet, safe space, gentle): they tested worst with simulated users. Button: "Join the waitlist", not "Save my spot".
Hand-drawn marks, v0.3 rules
Traced from the welcome screen: one continuous line that underlines the word, hooks, sweeps down and back, then curls into the next line. Tapered: thin ends, about 3.5px at its widest (on a 430pt screen), with a slight natural swell. Lime #92B44B, drawn as a filled shape. No ink wobble, no pen tip.
Motion, v0.3
Enter: rise 10px with 4px blur, 1.6s, cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1).
Line reveal: a soft edge (14px fade) wipes straight down, top to bottom. Never traced along the path. Slow pen pace, cubic-bezier(.42,.08,.5,.96): 2.2s for a long line, 1.5s for a short one.
Stars: pop in with a small overshoot (1s), then twinkle to 35% size and opacity, each on its own rhythm (1.8s, 2.5s, 3.2s).
Reduced motion: everything static.
Welcome sequence
One thing at a time: 1. the wordmark + “Welcome to FloraGrove” 2. stars 3. first line wipes down 4. “A grove is…” 5. second line, then the photo with the rest of the screen.
In the app: plays once (about 9s), tap to skip to the end.
On the landing page: loops slowly (about 15s, 4s hold, 1.2s fade).
Phone frame (marketing only)
Real iPhone Pro Max proportions: screen 430 × 932pt scaled into 342 × 741, 11px black bezel, outer 364 × 763, 56px outer radius, 45px screen radius, Dynamic Island, side buttons. It peeks: it rises from the bottom and the fold crops it. The screen is live text and vectors, never a screenshot.
