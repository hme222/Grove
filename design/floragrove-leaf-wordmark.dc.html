FloraGrove · Two weights, built into the word

The leaves become letters

The Two weights mark (an outlined leaf and a solid leaf) moved into the light/bold wordmark, the way the one-line G was the letter G. Each option is shown large, on cream, and at 24px and 16px to test legibility. The screen-reader name is still “FloraGrove”.

A · The v is the mark

The two leaves form the “v” in Grove: outlined leaf for light Flora, solid leaf for bold Grove. The app icon is that same v, so the word and the icon are one shape.

Logo designer
The strongest idea: the mark lives in the word, like the one-line G did.
Graphic designer
Stays readable down to 20px: the v shape survives even when the leaves blur.
Art director
Ownable and quiet. It doesn’t shout “plant app”, but anyone who looks twice sees it.
