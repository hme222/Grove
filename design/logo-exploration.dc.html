Ten marks for Grove

Each one is built only from the system: Lime on Night and Root, Cormorant Garamond for the name, the hand-drawn line and the star. Every mark is shown as a large mark, as an app icon at 110, 58 and 29px, as a 16px favicon, on cream, and as a lockup with the name. Scores are a judgement from three lenses (logo designer, graphic designer, art director), not test results.

Before you commit to any mark: "Grove" is used by other consumer brands, so run a trademark search on the name and the chosen mark in the app category.
